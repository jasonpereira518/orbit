# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People maintaining a personal professional network and pursuing introductions, referrals, mentors, and career opportunities.

## Product Purpose

Orbit helps users discover relevant people, start personalized conversations, and maintain relationships with evidence from their interactions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Outreach campaigns use one email or LinkedIn channel and typically contact 20–100 people. Users review outgoing messages. Connected Gmail and Outlook support sending and conversation sync; a locally enabled Chrome extension handles browser-only accounts. Prospects enter the main network after engagement or explicit saving.

Pro includes 250 research credits monthly; Lifetime includes 100 once. Users can supply personal provider keys. Research must distinguish evidence, uncertainty, and missing information.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the existing Orbit visual system, typography, theme tokens, and campaign history.

## Product Principles

- Show why a person is relevant and where the evidence comes from.
- Send the exact message the user approved from the account they selected.
- Distinguish an attempted action from a confirmed result.
- Preserve progress and make interruptions recoverable.
